# security

Security is taken seriously, but currently and in foreseeable future, there is no bounty program for this project.

## reporting a vulnerability

If you find something suspicious, I’d appreciate it if you could report it to me privately by email (it can be found on [my personal home page](https://luoji.bio/) or in the commit history — prefer the personal address instead of the institutional one).  I will give effort in responding to and acting on the reports, including, when the issue is not considered a vulnerability by me, explaining the reasoning.  However, given the nature of this project, I cannot make promise about how fast I will respond to or act on the report.  In addition, for complex legal reasons, I will *not* respond to or act on a report *during* work hours.
